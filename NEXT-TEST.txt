HOLY ORDER — READY FOR YOUR NEXT TEST
Build 0.4.0 / Road of Iron

Double-click click2play.desktop in this folder, then choose DEPLOY.
F11 toggles fullscreen. The original desert mission has click2play-desert.desktop.

WHAT CHANGED
- Longer covered convoy, an open rear, and your seat at the back for a clear view.
  Everyone keeps weapons down until dismounting.
- Twelve German MG positions. Trucks take actual bullet and wheel damage,
  attempt to pass obstacles, and dismount individually when stuck.
- The final ambush depends on who actually arrives. A single arriving truck
  gets no invented rear target; no arrivals means no authored ambush.
- Survivors split left/right, clear the ambush hills, then work back along both
  ridges. AI keeps its mission while reacting to nearby fire and using cover.
- Visible grenade windup/recovery for you and AI; repeated G presses cannot
  spam instant throws. AI rechecks nearby friendlies before release.
- C toggles crouch; Z toggles prone. Upright boots no longer show while crawling.
- Gentler hills, improved prone slope collision, deeper/wider shovel work,
  and much smaller grenade craters. Close blasts can still breach timber walls.
- Reduced repeated geometry, navigation, collision and rendering work.

QUICK CONTROLS
C crouch toggle / Z prone toggle / Q-E lean / G grenade / F interact
4 shovel: LMB digs deeper; RMB+LMB digs wider. J orders / Tab inventory.
You cannot shoot, throw grenades or walk around while seated in a moving truck.

84 automated mechanics checks passed, including a four-minute autonomous battle.
No soldier fell through terrain in that run. Real ammo, casualties and bodies

timing; other views and battle outcomes can perform differently.

STILL A PROTOTYPE
The procedural art and animations are rough. AI may hesitate around complex
rubble. Casualty balance needs your playtest; you and the whole force can die.
Soil supports finite pits and trenches, not tunnels or overhangs. Destruction
is budgeted, and there is no general structural collapse simulation.
