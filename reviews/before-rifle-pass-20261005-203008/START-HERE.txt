HOLY ORDER — THE FIRST DISPLACEMENT
Playable prototype / First Contact

Double-click click2play in this folder. Press BEGIN on the start screen.
You can also search for Holy Order in the application menu.

THE PREMISE
Your fictional late-medieval crusading order believed it had found the Ark
of the Covenant. The object was a device that scattered different groups
across different centuries. You arrive alone near the Capuzzo frontier in
Libya, on 14 June 1940, during the early British–Italian desert fighting.
The small outpost and individual encounter are fictional.

THE FIRST TEST
You start behind a large rock with a sword and steel armour. A short menu
explains the movement and melee controls. An isolated Italian sentry stands
well ahead of the other five defenders. Defeat him to trigger a brief rifle
pickup scene. His Carcano has six rounds loaded. Every body can be searched once for
three six-round Carcano en-bloc clips (18 reserve rounds), plus pocket lint.
Lint stacks, with no use yet. Compatible ammunition stacks and R reloads it.
Choose a prone crawl, crouched sneak or sprinting melee charge.
A single Italian grenade can be taken from the green crate on the left
approach to the outpost. It contains no ammunition.
Clear the remaining position using the rifle, sword, or steel gauntlets.
The final kill spawns a stopped British light truck at a fixed point on the
nearby dirt road. Follow the new marker and approach the patrol. They invite
you aboard and welcome you with a very questionable cup of tea. That short
subtitled scene ends at the test-complete screen. The map and J objective
menu track the sentry, outpost and road rendezvous.

CONTROLS
WASD          Move
Mouse         Look
Left click    Fire / sword strike / punch; hold and release for heavy melee
Right click   Aim down rifle sights; with melee + sprint, crazy charge
Shift         Sprint
Q / E         Lean left / right
Space         Jump; mantle or climb reachable low cover
C             Crouch
Z             Toggle prone crawl
1             Sword (rifle slung)
2             Rifle (sword sheathed)
3             Armoured fists (both weapons stowed)
R             Reload from looted reserve ammunition
F             Interact / search pockets / take grenade
G             Throw grenade, if acquired
Tab           Inventory
J             Quest objectives
Esc           Pause / resume
F11           Fullscreen / windowed

DESIGN NOTES
The knight's full plate, sabatons and steel gauntlets are inspired by
late-medieval equipment, around 1450. The order is fictional. The Carcano
M91 and six-round capacity suit the Italian setting; the models are original,
simplified silhouettes, not museum-quality replicas. British patrol models
have broad-brimmed helmets and simplified SMLE-style rifle silhouettes.
Steel armour does not make the player immune to 20th-century rifle fire.
Damage values, one-hit sword/rifle takedowns and movement are game balancing.
The Breda M35 prop uses simplified impact detonation with a game failsafe.
The patrol vehicle is a Morris CS8-inspired early-war British light truck,
appropriate to the June 1940 setting. Reloading conserves rounds and is an
FPS abstraction rather than a detailed clip-handling simulation.

No voice acting, multiplayer, external accounts or paid assets are needed.
Everything is local. No system packages were installed. This prototype does
not save campaign progress; retrying begins a fresh encounter. Mouse and
keyboard are supported; controller mapping is not included in this version.
