HOLY ORDER 0.6 — ONE LAUNCHER / LIGHTER BATTLES

START HERE
Close an already-running older game, then open click2play.desktop. Choose
Winter, Desert or Omaha. Esc > Mission Menu returns to the selector. Only one
mission is loaded at a time. A shared launch lock prevents separate shortcuts
from starting simultaneous copies. Installed legacy shortcuts now open this
same menu, highlighting their previous mission. The duplicate click2play files
in this game folder were archived in reviews/before-unified-performance-20261006.
No external software, engine or asset download was performed for this update.

The full 600-American / 100-German force, 20 boats, 32 defensive MG positions,

GRAPHICS AND SIMULATION
Low / Max FPS is the default: 50% 3D resolution, no MSAA, no sun shadows,
no SSAO or volumetric fog, 450-metre camera limit, cheaper effects, fewer
full-detail soldiers and batched building / vehicle / distant soldier visuals.
The UI renders at full resolution. The existing engine uses its Mobile renderer.
F6 cycles Low (50%), Standard (75%) and High (100%, short sun shadows).
F10 hides or shows the FPS counter. Frames are capped at 60.

Fixed simulation is 30 Hz in Winter and Desert, and 20 Hz in Low Omaha (30 Hz
in Standard/High). Physics interpolation fills the rendered movement between
ticks. Mouse rotation and weapon animation run on rendered frames. Stable
soldier-instance slots prevent interpolating from one soldier into another.
AI decisions are distributed across rendered frames; movement and bullets still
run throughout the battle. Gunshot audio is pooled, bullet marks are bounded,
and small-arms hits no longer request expensive mesh booleans. The equipment
collision and damage behavior remain intact.

GRENADES AND WEAPONS
Grenades make modest shallow soil scars and localized holes in thin wood,
sandbags and town walls. Remaining pieces retain collision; thick bunker concrete
and winter truck armor stay strong. Small loose slivers are removed so a breach
can be crouched through. Only nearby terrain cells gain finer geometry after a
cut, and geometry updates are queued to limit bursts of expensive work.
Automatic weapons retain fractional shot timing between ticks. Representative
historical automatic rates and the separate semi-auto / manual rifle timing are
listed, with references, in WEAPON-RATES.txt.
The knight still starts with 300 health in all missions. Winter's trucks retain
the 4,500-health chassis, protected wheels and reinforced passenger compartment.

CPU COMPARISON
An isolated 45-second post-landing replay took 56.7 wall seconds before
and 24.5 after (56.8% less time). Both used the same fixed seed and
force size, 60 simulated render frames/sec, and the bundled engine headlessly.
Median render-loop interval: 19.45 -> 2.46 ms; 95th percentile:
40.09 -> 35.05 ms. Tick rate and battle outcomes differ as intended.
These are CPU-side measurements, NOT desktop FPS. Expensive frames still occur.
The new graphics preset cannot be GPU-benchmarked from this restricted session.
See reviews/performance-update.json for both complete reports and limitations.

MEMORY
RAM holds the running game, world and simulation data. VRAM holds graphics data
such as textures, meshes and render buffers. AI, targeting and collisions also
cost CPU time, which is why simply reducing textures is not enough for hundreds
of soldiers. One loaded mission and fewer terrain/visual resources reduce both
memory pressure and ongoing work. Recent desktop logs identify the RTX 3070
Laptop GPU; live VRAM usage is unavailable in this execution session.
