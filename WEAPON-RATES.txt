HOLY ORDER 0.6 — WEAPON TIMING

Automatic cyclic rates, while a burst is firing:
  M1918A2 BAR:       375 rounds/minute (its slow automatic setting)
  Browning M1919A4:  500 rounds/minute
  Browning M2 HB:    500 rounds/minute
  MP 40:            500 rounds/minute
  MG 34:            800 rounds/minute
  MG 42:          1,200 rounds/minute
  Breda Modello 37:  450 rounds/minute

These are representative rates within the documented ranges, not sustained
rates including pauses and reloads. AI still fires bursts, aims, takes cover,
reloads and consumes actual ammunition. Fractional shot time now carries into
the next simulation tick instead of being discarded, preventing a lower frame
rate from quietly slowing every automatic weapon.

Semiautomatic rifles do not have one fixed automatic rate. Garand and M1 Carbine
accept separate trigger presses up to five per second; the G43 accepts four.
These are gameplay trigger limits, not historical measured cyclic rates.
AI aiming delays remain separate (1.0 / 0.9 / 1.1 seconds respectively).

Kar98k and Carcano are manually cycled: a two-second minimum between shots,
with further AI aiming time and real reload pauses. Manual cycling depends on
the operator; these are representative animation timings, not an exact physical
constant for every shooter. The flamethrower uses damage/fuel pulses at 0.16 s,
not a fictional rounds-per-minute specification. Grenades, the sword, fists and
the shovel retain their windups and recovery times.

Historical references used for the automatic rates:
- US War Department TM-E 30-451, Handbook on German Military Forces, 1945,
  Chapter VII, small arms: MP 40, MG 34 and MG 42.
  https://www.ibiblio.org/hyperwar/Germany/HB/HB-7.html
- National Army Museum MG42 collection entry, documented 1,200–1,500 rpm.
  https://collection.nam.ac.uk/detail.php?acc=1964-05-60-12
- US Army FM 17-20, 1957, small-arms characteristics (Browning .30 and .50).
  https://generalstaff.org/MAN/USA/FM017-20_1957.pdf
- US War Department FM 23-15, Browning Automatic Rifle M1918A2.
  https://www.90thidpg.us/Reference/Manuals/FM%2023-15.pdf
  Army Historical Foundation's explanation of the two automatic settings:
  https://armyhistory.org/browning-automatic-rifle/
- US Intelligence Bulletin, November 1942, Weapons Most Frequently Used (Italy),
  Breda Model 37: 450 rpm maximum, 20-round charger.
  https://lonesentry.com/articles/it_weapons/index.html

Validation: tests/unified_test.gd exercises actual AI weapon firing at 20, 30, 60
and 120 simulation ticks/sec and checks both cadence and ammunition accounting.
